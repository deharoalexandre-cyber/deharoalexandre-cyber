# Elyne Corp

**Local AI and cybersecurity, developed in France.**  
[Français](README.md) · [Product catalog](https://elynecorp.com/) · [Contact](mailto:admin@elynecorp.fr)

Our catalog presents **16 products and variants** organized around two technology lines: **Elyne**, for AI applications and agentic security, and **TrustID**, for workstation and identity trust. [EIE](https://github.com/deharoalexandre-cyber/EIE) is our open-source local inference engine. The sixteen catalog cards do not represent sixteen unrelated codebases.

**Proprietary documentation repositories: public viewing, no reuse license.** Among the products in the table, EIE remains the open-source exception; each repository's notice states its terms.

## Reading the evidence

- [EAS Defense: architecture and qualifications](https://github.com/deharoalexandre-cyber/EAS-Defense); [detailed OWASP Agentic 2026 map](https://github.com/deharoalexandre-cyber/EAS-Defense/blob/main/docs/owasp-agentic-2026.en.md). The 86 measures are an **internal** decomposition; the evidence ends with the bounded R48 laboratory test and remains tied to the tested versions.
- [EIE: source, documentation, and validation](https://github.com/deharoalexandre-cyber/EIE). Performance results depend on the models, hardware, and settings stated there.
- [Elyne Benchmark](https://github.com/deharoalexandre-cyber/elyne-benchmark): a public protocol and results, distinct from individual product qualifications.

The presentation repositories contain architecture and scoped evidence summaries. They do not expose proprietary product source code, operational policies, or raw laboratory logs. [Ownership notice](NOTICE.md). For an accompanied evaluation: [admin@elynecorp.fr](mailto:admin@elynecorp.fr).
